# Photos on actions and damage reports

Kept out of the MVP for storage, cost, consent and moderation (requirements §5),
not because nobody wants them. The two places they would earn their keep:

- **After an action** — the before-and-after, which is the only reason anyone
  looks at a past work day.
- **On a damaged tool** — a photo settles what "damaged" means far better than a
  free-text note.

Needs R2, an upload path that does not go through the worker's CPU budget, a
consent line in the privacy policy, and a rule about who may delete what. Doing
the second one alone is a much smaller task than doing both.
