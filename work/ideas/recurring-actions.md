# Recurring actions

Rejected for the MVP in favour of "duplicate action" (ADR-013). Recurrence rules
bring per-occurrence exceptions, "edit this or all future", and reminder
scheduling against a series — one of the largest hidden costs in scheduling
software.

Revisit only if duplication proves insufficient in practice, and only with a
concrete example of what people are actually doing: "every second Saturday in
spring" is a different feature from "the same crew, four times a year".
