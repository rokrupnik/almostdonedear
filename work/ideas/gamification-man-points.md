# Gamification — "nabiranje moških točk"

Explicitly not a favour ledger (ADR-017): no credits, no debts, nothing that
turns generosity into accounting between friends. A light, general scheme, played
for laughs rather than for fairness.

Derivable from data we already record — attendance is stored from day one exactly
so this needs no migration when it happens. What it must not become: a number
that makes someone feel billed for missing a Saturday.

Worth designing only after a group has used the app for a season, when there is
real attendance data to look at and a real sense of whether it would be funny or
tiresome.
