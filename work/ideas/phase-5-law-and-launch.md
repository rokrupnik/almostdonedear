# Phase 5 — law, launch, first real moba

~4 dev-days, `docs/roadmap.md`.

- Privacy policy and terms in Slovenian, naming Cloudflare and Resend as
  processors (FR-43). Two pages of text; their absence is the problem, not their
  length.
- Self-service JSON export (FR-40) and account deletion as anonymisation
  (FR-41) — the name becomes "Nekdanji član", the email goes, the history stays
  so other people's actions still make sense.
- The 24-month inactivity sweep (FR-42).
- Cloudflare Web Analytics: cookieless, so there is no consent banner to build.
- A D1 backup exported to R2 **and restored once**, because an untested backup is
  not a backup.

Exit signal: a real work day organised end to end in the app, with WhatsApp used
only for chatting and for sharing the link.
